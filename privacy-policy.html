🫧 Privacy Policy - Bubble Wrap Game

Last Updated: April 28, 2026

Summary: Bubble Wrap is a simple, fun game that respects your privacy. We don't collect, store, or share any personal information. All game data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Bubble Wrap does NOT collect any personal information from users. We do not:

  • Collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Track your location
  • Access your photos, contacts, or other personal files
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Create user accounts or profiles

2. Local Data Storage

The game stores the following data locally on your device only using browser localStorage:

  • Game Settings: Theme preferences, sound/vibration settings, language choice, bubble size
  • Game Statistics: Total bubbles popped, games completed, best completion time

Important: This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

3. Third-Party Advertising

Bubble Wrap displays advertisements through Google AdSense/AdMob. These services may collect certain information to provide relevant ads:

  • Google AdSense/AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies to show personalized ads
  • Google's advertising partners may collect device information and usage data
  • For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ad Settings

4. Children's Privacy

Bubble Wrap is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age. The game:

  • Does not contain social features or chat
  • Does not request personal information
  • Does not include external links (except in this privacy policy)
  • Complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

5. Data Security

Since we don't collect or transmit personal data, there is minimal security risk. All game data is stored locally on your device and can be cleared by:

  • Using the "Clear Stats" button in game settings
  • Clearing your browser data/cache
  • Uninstalling the app (mobile)

6. Permissions

The game may request the following device permissions:

  • Vibration: To provide haptic feedback when popping bubbles (optional, can be disabled)
  • Local Storage: To save game settings and statistics on your device

No other permissions are required or requested.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete from our servers. You have full control over locally stored data on your device.
